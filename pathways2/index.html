What this is

A thinking tool, not official advice. It helps you see what is possible and what you could do next. It does not know your grades, it does not predict them, and it will never tell you that something is closed to you.

Check with a person

Confirm anything that matters with your school's Education and Career Guidance counsellor, and at moe.gov.sg.

If you are that counsellor, start here.
