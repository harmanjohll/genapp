## Provenance discipline

The tools tag every figure and the tags mean something specific. Keep this if you extend anything.

- **Measured** comes from the workbook here, or from the MOE School Finder record. 1,148 calendar events between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2025.
- **Modelled** is an adjustable control inside the tool that uses it. Never presented as a measurement.
- **Researched** is from published sources outside Beatty.
- **Unverified** is not published anywhere reachable. The house colours for Harwood, Sturdee and Fisher are the standing example.

## Known limits of the workbook

Three, and they bind every conclusion drawn from it.

Counts are recorded occurrences, not headcount and not contact hours. An entry covering a whole level counts once, as does an entry covering four named Beattyians.

Attendance is out of scope by instruction. Every claim about who is overcommitted is inferred from selection language in the card text, not from participation records.

Where a department left the card blank, the activity is invisible here. Under-recording is likely and the direction of that bias is unknown.

## One correction carried forward

`REPORT.md` section 3.2 credits Boon Lay Secondary with "domain stones, where points accumulate separately by domain and the top house in each domain wins that domain's stone". That could not be re-sourced. What is findable is that Boon Lay runs four gemstone-named houses, Garnet, Emerald, Morganite and Sapphire, each owning four CCAs, from a CCA-centric model dating to 2017.

Two consequences. The per-domain scoring mechanic behind the DREAM recommendation needs a different citation or none. And Boon Lay is a working Singapore instance of the CCA-anchored architecture the report rejects on four counts, which makes a call to them the cheapest piece of evidence still available to this project.
