# A House System on the Wednesday and Thursday Band
### What two years of calendar data says, what Gagné and Renzulli license, and what Beatty can actually build

Beatty Secondary School · Enrichment architecture paper · August 2026
Evidence base: 1,148 calendar events from beattyadmin, 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025, decomposed into 1,380 activity records, 605 of them genuine enrichment across 133 named programmes.

---

## TLDR

**The band is half built and offers no choice.** Cards were issued on 82 of roughly 160 available Wednesday and Thursday term days, none at all in October, November or December in either year. A given level, on a given band day, was offered a mean of 1.33 activities. On 46 percent of level days it was offered nothing.

**Most programmes never develop anything.** Of 133 programmes, 75 ran on one or two dates. Only 29 ran six times or more. Gagné dismisses enrichment offered two or three times a month as busywork; Beatty's sustained programmes average gaps of 20 to 51 days, which sits at or below that line.

**A quarter of the protected band is not enrichment.** Oral examinations take 41 entries, the Secondary 1 ICT training series 31, and seven cards were issued with nothing recorded at all. Mathematics olympiad, at 33, is the largest genuine occupant.

**Overcommitment is real and precisely located.** Only 26 programmes are selective, and they recruit from the same pool. Secondary 3 in Term 1 of 2024 had six selective programmes live at once. The fix is to make substitutable programmes collide on purpose, because two activities at the same hour cannot both claim the same Beattyian.

**The single best piece of news is in your own calendar.** Beatty's CCA, uniformed group and performing arts activity falls on Tuesday (29), Saturday (29), Friday (26) and Monday (18). Wednesday and Thursday carry 12 each. Nationally Wednesday is the busiest CCA day in Singapore secondary schools; at Beatty it is nearly the emptiest. **The band you want to protect is already free.** That removes the largest change management risk before you start.

**The recommendation.** Five houses, labelled A to E for now, **class anchored and vertical**: every form class from Secondary 1 to 5 permanently belongs to a house, so house membership rides on the class list you already produce at Secondary 1 registration. Wednesday becomes the House Band for exploration and skill building, everyone included. Thursday becomes the Pathway Band, cross house and interest led, where a talent pool of 15 to 20 percent revolves in and out of sustained investigation. Three cycles a year of ten sessions. Term 4 released to the examination calendar by design.

**The club catalogue is not invented.** Twenty nine clubs, each traced to programmes that actually ran. Sixteen can be converted almost as they stand because they already have cadence and survived both years. Eleven exist but are too thin to develop anything. Only two are genuinely new.

**Two warnings.** First, a house is a milieu, not a developmental process. If houses become the unit of talent grouping you cap every field's talent pool at one fifth of the cohort, which inverts both models. Houses own belonging and exploration; fields own pursuit. Second, do not map the five houses onto the five DREAM values. It is irresistibly neat and it is trait sorting, which is exactly what the evidence warns against. Use DREAM as the five scoring domains instead, so every house competes on all five.

## Part 1 · What the calendar actually says

### 1.1 The band operates at half capacity and dies before the year ends

Eighty three cards on 82 distinct dates across two years, against a theoretical ceiling near 160. Cards appear January through September and then stop: not one in October, November or December in either year, and June nearly empty.

Either Term 4 is genuinely consumed by the national examination calendar, in which case say so and design a three cycle year, or the band is abandoned when pressure rises, in which case it is not protected time. The data cannot separate these. The leadership decision can.

The examination calendar supports the first reading. In 2026, O Level Mother Tongue falls on 2 June, orals run mid July to early August, prelims late August, N Level written from mid September, O Level written 19 October to 10 November, and schools used as examination venues end Term 4 on 23 October. Upper secondary is realistically available from Term 1 to mid Term 3, roughly 22 to 24 usable weeks.

### 1.2 Beattyians are not choosing

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean distinct programmes per card | 2.9 |
| Cards offering exactly one programme | 19 of 75 |
| Possible level slots (card dates × 5 levels) | 375 |
| Level slots with any offering at all | 201 (54 percent) |
| Mean offerings per level per band day | 1.33 |
| Level days carrying two or more options | 36 of 161 (22 percent) |

A mean of 1.33 is not a menu, it is an allocation. Renzulli's canonical cluster mechanic asks students to rank five choices and bars repeats across cycles. At 1.33, the encounter is scheduled for them.

This also means the school has no overcommitment problem *inside* the band, because there is nothing to over choose. The problem sits in 1.5.

### 1.5 The overcommitment mechanism, located precisely

One hundred and twenty of 605 enrichment occurrences carry selection language, drawn from just 26 programmes. Selective programmes competing for the same level in the same term average 2.3 and peak at 6.

The Beattyian selected for SMO is very likely the Beattyian selected for MUN, for Tech Champs, for Tier Three leadership. Nothing in the calendar suggests anyone was reconciling those claims.

## Part 2 · The construct, and what it does and does not license

### 2.1 Gagné: the Differentiating Model of Giftedness and Talent

Gagné separates **giftedness**, untrained natural ability across mental and physical domains, from **talent**, systematically developed competence in a field. The bridge is the **developmental process**: informal and formal learning, and above all sustained practice understood as an investment of time, energy and money. Two catalyst families modulate it. **Intrapersonal**: motivation, volition, self management, awareness. **Environmental**: milieu, significant individuals, and provisions meaning enrichment, grouping and acceleration. Chance runs through all of it. The prevalence threshold is the top ten percent of the relevant reference group.

What this licenses:

- Houses are an **environmental catalyst**, supplying milieu and significant individuals. Gagné would recognise that as real value.
- Houses do **not** constitute a developmental process. Belonging to a house develops nothing. Only sustained structured practice in a field does.
- His four enrichment types are **density, difficulty, depth, diversity**. A band that only adds diversity has used one of four.
- Cadence is the mechanism, not a scheduling convenience.

### 2.2 Renzulli: the Schoolwide Enrichment Model

The Three Ring conception holds that gifted behaviours arise from the interaction of above average ability, task commitment and creativity, and appear **in some people, at some times, under some circumstances**. Giftedness is behaviour, not a permanent property of a child.

The Enrichment Triad supplies the grammar:

- **Type I, general exploratory experiences.** Deliberate exposure to fields and people outside the curriculum. Broad, low commitment, whole population.
- **Type II, group training activities.** Cognitive and affective process skills, research method, learning how to investigate. Medium commitment, offered widely.
- **Type III, individual and small group investigation of real problems.** The student as firsthand inquirer, producing a real product for a real audience. Few students, self chosen, high commitment.

Two further components matter here.

**Enrichment clusters** are nongraded groups formed around shared interest, meeting in a designated block, facilitated rather than taught, producing something for an audience beyond the classroom. Renzulli's own recommended block is Wednesday or Friday afternoons. Typical size is 5 to 15, up to 25 with a second adult. Published cycle length is 8 to 10 weekly sessions, two cycles a year, with a catalogue refreshed each cycle. Facilitators are recruited by open call to teachers, support staff, parents and community members, and **content expertise is explicitly not required**.

**Revolving Door identification** maintains a talent pool of roughly 15 to 20 percent, constituted from achievement data, teacher nomination, and assessments of creativity and task commitment, and refreshed by **action information**, the signal generated when a student becomes visibly excited about a topic. Students revolve into Type III on demonstrated interest and out on project completion.

What this forbids:

- Type III is not a harder Type II. If Thursday becomes teacher directed advanced content, the construct has collapsed and you have renamed your extension classes.
- Fixed talent streams violate the Three Ring conception.
- A cluster with no external audience is a hobby period. Renzulli's four authenticity tests are personal relevance, **no predetermined solution**, creation of new product or information, and an audience who **voluntarily attend**.

### 2.3 The fidelity guardrail

Gagné explains *why* cadence and provision matter. Renzulli explains *what* to put in the block and *how* students enter and leave it.

> A house supplies milieu and mentors. A field supplies practice and mastery. Give houses the exploration and the belonging. Give fields the pursuit. If a Beattyian can only do robotics because their house runs robotics, the design has capped every talent pool at twenty percent of the cohort and inverted both models at once.

### 2.4 Honesty about the evidence

Three things should be said plainly to staff and to the SMC.

There is **no randomised evidence that house systems raise attainment**. The largest study of structural reorganisation, covering 119 funded high schools, found no significant achievement trends but did find increased extracurricular participation, higher promotion rates and fewer violent incidents. Adopt a house system as a culture and participation structure, not as an attainment intervention.

Enrichment effect sizes are **softer than they look**. A recent umbrella review puts enrichment effects on academic achievement at 0.68, collapsing to 0.32 after correction for publication bias, and the largest effects come from intensive residential formats rather than weekly in school bands.

**Neither Renzulli nor Gagné appears in MOE guidance.** Singapore's language is school based talent development and higher ability learners. If you cite these models internally you are importing frameworks, not aligning to national policy. Frame accordingly.

## Part 3 · The house model

### 3.1 The four candidate structures

| Model | How members are assigned | Change load | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class anchored vertical** | Every form class Sec 1 to 5 permanently belongs to a house; membership rides on class allocation | Low | **Recommended** |
| Randomly sorted vertical | Random at Sec 1, balanced by gender, banding and CCA, siblings together | Medium | Best pedagogically, adds an administrative layer |
| Faculty aligned | Each house owns a domain | High | Do not use, it inverts the construct |
| Interest guilds, reselected yearly | Students choose their house annually | High | Destroys the five year mentoring relationship |

**Recommendation: class anchored vertical.** Kranji Secondary integrated house allocation with class allocation in 2022 alongside Full Subject Based Banding, so Class 1K sits in Kestrel House. Hua Yi Secondary, the closest five house precedent in Singapore, states that every class from Secondary 1 to 5 is associated with one of five houses. Both are working models in schools like yours.

It also survives Full Subject Based Banding cleanly. Under FSBB only six common curriculum subjects hold a form class together, roughly a third of curriculum time; English, Mother Tongue, Mathematics, Science and Humanities scatter students into G1, G2 and G3 subject classes. Your calendar's G1, G2 and G3 columns are **subject blocks, not form classes**. A house keyed to form class is therefore stable while a house keyed to banding would fragment.

The honest cost: house composition inherits class composition, and mixing is weaker than random assignment would give. Accept that in year one, and revisit once the structure holds.

### 3.2 The Harry Potter problem, and the DREAM trap

Sorting students into fixed identity houses with strong trait archetypes carries a specific risk, and the evidence is more nuanced than the folklore.

The relevant experiment assigned 112 children to coloured teams for two weeks. Positive in group bias emerged in every condition, meaning arbitrary group markers alone produce favouritism. Negative out group bias appeared **only where adults actively emphasised team identity**, and only among six and seven year olds; it had faded by nine or ten. Your students are 13 to 17, so the direct risk is lower than the story suggests. The transferable lesson is that **teacher behaviour, not the sorting, is what makes group identity socially consequential.**

Separately, sorting on *attributes* is closer to setting than to houses, and the Education Endowment Foundation rates setting and streaming at zero months progress overall, with negative effects on the attitudes and engagement of lower attainers.

Which brings us to the trap. Beatty has five DREAM values: Discipline, Resilience, Empathy, Adaptability, Mindfulness. Five values, five houses. The mapping is irresistible and it should be resisted. Assigning a Beattyian to Discipline House tells them what they are, and tells the other four houses what they are not.

**The better move.** Keep DREAM as the **five scoring domains** of the House Cup. Every house competes across all five, every term. This borrows the strongest points design found in Singapore practice, Boon Lay's domain stones, where points accumulate separately by domain and the top house in each domain wins that domain's stone. It gives DREAM real operational teeth, it prevents one strong domain carrying a house, and it keeps identity and character separate.

### 3.3 Naming

Five options, weighed:

| Scheme | Example | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Navigational stars** | Polaris, Vega, Sirius, Canopus, Antares | **Recommended.** Inherits the NEXUS tagline *Exploring New Skies*. Naval heritage from Admiral Beatty. Navigation is the literal image of a Purpose Driven Steward. Trait neutral, achievement neutral, will not date. |
| Colour first, name second | Navy, Gold, Crimson, Jade, Slate | Safe. Colour is the one element that survives renaming. But colour alone gives students nothing to belong to. |
| DREAM values | Discipline, Resilience, Empathy… | Neat and wrong. Trait sorting. Use as scoring domains instead. |
| Founders and principals | Meyer and successors | Traditional, common locally, but named person houses carry ongoing reputational exposure. One school needed an audited referendum to unwind a single name. |
| Hive and bee heritage | five hives | The bee is genuinely yours, from Admiral Beatty's coat of arms. Risks reading as juvenile at upper secondary, and five bee variants are hard to tell apart. |

Whatever you choose, **assign the colour first and treat it as permanent**. Schools that renamed houses kept the colour. Colour is also the operational anchor: PE attire is issued by house colour at Secondary 1 registration across Singapore schools, which makes it the cheapest possible identity carrier.

Label the houses A to E in planning documents until students name them. Student naming is the cheapest legitimacy purchase available, and both the American case study and Hua Yi's 2020 refresh used it.

### 3.4 The shape of the week

**Wednesday, 3.00 to 6.00 · House Band.** Vertical clusters within house. Secondary 1 to 5 together. Everyone participates. Renzulli Type I and Type II: exploration and the process skills that make later pursuit possible. Five houses running in parallel is the floor; with clusters of 25 across a house of about 160, each house runs six or seven clusters, giving roughly 32 across the school.

**Thursday, 3.00 to 6.00 · Pathway Band.** Cross house, organised by field. Tiered. Type II advanced and Type III: sustained practice, investigation, product, audience. The talent pool revolves in and out by cycle. Elective, so roughly 55 to 65 percent of the cohort attends, needing about 20 clusters.

**The year.** Three cycles of ten sessions, aligned to terms. Cycle 1 in Term 1, Cycle 2 in Term 2, Cycle 3 in Term 3 to prelims. Term 4 released. Ten sessions clears Renzulli's published floor of eight; three cycles gives 30 Wednesdays and 30 Thursdays, which is 60 band days against the 41 a year Beatty already manages. Ambitious but not fanciful.

### 3.5 The tiers

| Tier | Renzulli | Gagné | Who | Cadence | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Explore** | Type I | Catalyst activation | All Beattyians | One cycle per taster | An encounter and a recorded interest |
| **Build** | Type II | Formal learning, early investment | Roughly half by election | Ten sessions per cycle | Demonstrated skill |
| **Pursue** | Type III | Sustained practice | 15 to 20 percent, revolving | Multiple cycles, mentor attached | Real product, external audience |

Sizing. A Pursue tier at 15 to 20 percent of about 1,000 Beattyians is 150 to 200 places. Today the school runs 26 selective programmes generating roughly 60 occurrences a year. This is the growth edge, and it is exactly where the most able Beattyians are currently being pulled six ways at once.

### 3.6 What houses own, and what they do not

Houses own belonging, vertical mentoring, the Wednesday exploration carousel, inter house competition on the five DREAM domains, and the Total Talent Portfolio conversation with a house tutor who follows a Beattyian for five years.

Houses do not own the Thursday pathway rosters, Type III mentor allocation, or entry to any field. A Beattyian in any house can pursue any field. Keep House Cup points to the Wednesday band, to sport and to performance. The moment a house earns points for its members' olympiad results, the talent pool fragments into five.

### 3.7 Coexistence with CCA and LEAPS 2.0

This matters more than any pedagogical point, because it is what parents will ask about.

CCA is compulsory from Secondary 1. LEAPS 2.0 grades Participation, Achievement, Leadership and Service, and Participation requires two to five unbroken years of school based CCA at 75 percent attendance or more, converting to one or two bonus points for post secondary admission. **A house band that displaces CCA hours threatens a countable, parent visible outcome.**

Three rules follow.

The house band is framed against the **Enrichment** strand of LEAPS, which carries no numerical level, and it feeds **Leadership** and **Service**. It is never presented as a CCA substitute.

The **House Committee is constituted as a leadership CCA**, which is recognised practice in Singapore, so a house exco earns Leadership credit legitimately.

**House service runs through VIA**, which the data already supports: the VIA Project is the highest frequency enrichment programme in the whole dataset at 27 dates and 29 occurrences, 93 percent of it already inside the band, with Toa Payoh Care Corner as the recurring partner.

And the structural reason this works at Beatty specifically: Wednesday and Thursday already carry 12 CCA occurrences each against Tuesday's 29 and Saturday's 29. You are not taking CCA time. You are filling days that are already quiet.

## Part 5 · Concurrency, the load management instrument

### 5.1 The rule

**Substitutable activities collide. Complementary activities separate.**

Two clubs recruiting the same student profile run at the same hour, so a Beattyian must choose one. Two clubs developing different capacities run in different blocks, so a Beattyian can hold both.

### 5.2 The profiles

Every club in the catalogue carries a recruiting profile: analytic, discursive, inquiry, creative, service, physical, global, career. A profile appearing in more than one block is a **leak**, because it lets one Beattyian be claimed twice. The configurator computes leaks live.

### 5.3 The collision sets

**Set A, analytical pursuit** (Thursday): Mathematics Olympiad Guild, Science Olympiad Guild, NEXUS Applied STEM Studio, Data and AI Craft.

**Set B, discursive pursuit** (Thursday, same block as A): Model UN Delegation, Debate and Oracy Society, Policy and Enterprise Forum.

Sets A and B run against each other deliberately, because both draw the academically able and both are talent formation in Gagné's sense. A Beattyian doing SMO cannot also do MUN in the same cycle. Across three cycles they can do both, sequentially, with proper depth in each. That is the point.

**Set C, inquiry and global** (Thursday, second block if you run two): Geographical Investigation Unit, Junior Historical Society, Global Exchange Corps, Industry and Work Exposure.

Leadership, service and health move to **Wednesday inside the house block**. Peer Support Corps, House Leadership Cadre, House Service Corps, Lighten Up, House Sport League. This single move resolves most of the Secondary 3 pile up observed in 1.5, because leadership and academic pursuit stop competing for the same afternoon.

### 5.4 The commitment ceiling

One Wednesday house commitment. At most one Thursday pathway commitment. Plus CCA on its own days, which at Beatty means Tuesday, Friday, Monday and Saturday.

Maximum two concurrent enrichment commitments. Enforced through the timetable rather than through exhortation, which is why it will hold.

## Part 6 · Change management

The Education Endowment Foundation's implementation guidance sets four stages, Explore, Prepare, Deliver, Sustain, and says complex whole school initiatives need at least two years. Its named failure modes map uncomfortably well onto this proposal: initiative overload, the implementation dip, undefined active ingredients, over reliance on individuals, insufficient infrastructure, and neglected de implementation.

### 6.1 What makes this cheap at Beatty

Four things, all from your own records.

**Wednesday and Thursday are already your emptiest days.** 12 CCA occurrences each against 29 on Tuesday and Saturday.

**The band already exists** and staff already receive a card for it. You are changing what fills it, not creating a new institution.

**Sixteen of 29 clubs already run** with cadence and two year persistence. The first cycle can be built almost entirely from things that already work.

**House can ride on class allocation**, which you already produce at Secondary 1 registration, following Kranji and Hua Yi.

### 6.2 What makes it expensive

**Facilitator supply is the binding constraint.** A Wednesday house band for Secondary 1 to 4 at clusters of 25 needs roughly 32 clusters running at once. Beatty currently mounts a mean of 2.9 programmes per band day. That is a tenfold increase in simultaneous adult deployment. Renzulli's model does not require content expertise from facilitators and explicitly recruits parents, support staff and community members, which is the only way this arithmetic closes.

**The de implementation question.** EEF says you must explicitly stop something. The 75 programmes that ran once or twice are the obvious candidates, and retiring them is also the fastest way to free adults.

**Teacher buy in is the measured variance.** The one house system dissertation found 25 percent teacher disengagement directly producing performance disparities between houses.

### 6.3 A sequence

**Term 4 this year, Explore.** Publish the audit. Let departments see the 1.33 figure and the ten empty coverage cells. Ask each department which of its programmes it wants to convert, rebuild or retire. Name the five houses with students.

**Term 1 next year, Prepare and pilot.** Run one cycle of the Wednesday house band only, with the 16 convert clubs, at Secondary 1 to 3. Do not touch Thursday. Do not touch Secondary 4 and 5. Publish the fill rate: the percentage of Beattyians receiving their first or second choice. If it drops below 80 percent, the catalogue is too narrow.

**Term 2 and 3, Deliver.** Add the Thursday pathway band and the Pursue tier for a first cohort of about 60, not 150. Attach a named mentor to each Type III. Audit at week three of each cycle: does every Type III have a named external audience in its Management Plan?

**Year two, Sustain and extend.** Add Secondary 4. Rebuild the eleven thin clubs. Introduce the House Cup on the five DREAM domains with per capita scoring and a termly reset.

### 6.4 Three fidelity audits you can run yourself

**The audience audit.** By week three of every cycle, what percentage of Pursue projects name a real external person or body who will voluntarily attend? Target 100 percent. This is the specific failure mode the published fidelity study found.

**The predetermined solution test.** Ask every facilitator: do you already know what your students will produce? If yes, it is a mini course, not a cluster.

**The dosage test.** Sessions per month per club. Below three, Gagné calls it busywork, and you are running an exposure programme rather than a talent development one. Say which you are running.

## Part 7 · What breaks this

**Oral examinations reclaim the band.** Forty one entries over two years say they will. Publish the oral windows before cycles are set and shorten Cycle 2 around them.

**Facilitator supply fails and the model shrinks back to 1.33 options.** The most likely single failure.

**Type III becomes Type II in disguise.** The commonest failure of enrichment model implementations worldwide.

**Houses colonise pathways.** The moment a house earns points for members' olympiad results, the talent pool fragments into five.

**The invisible curriculum stays invisible.** Ten empty coverage cells may reflect recording failure rather than provision failure. Fixing recording discipline is a precondition, not an afterthought.

**Secondary 4 and 5 get written off.** The data shows this already happening. A vertical house that stops at Secondary 3 is not vertical.

## Appendix · Method and limits

Every event in the beattyadmin calendar from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025 was enumerated and its body opened where it fell on a Wednesday or Thursday or carried an activity signal. Times were converted from UTC to Singapore time before day of week was assigned. The band card is a Level by banding group table, so one card yields up to fifteen records; 83 cards produced 315 entries. Classification uses a ten category scheme, of which categories 1 to 9 are enrichment and category 10 marks time inside the band consumed by something else. An adversarial verification pass checked timezone conversion across all 1,148 events, confirmed no event was dropped, and audited a random sample of classifications.

Three limits bind the conclusions.

Counts are recorded occurrences, not headcount and not contact hours. An entry covering a whole level counts once, as does an entry covering four named Beattyians.

Attendance is out of scope by instruction. Every claim about who is overcommitted is inferred from selection language in the card text, not from participation records.

Where a department left the card blank, the activity is invisible here. Under recording is likely and the direction of that bias is unknown.
