Hood, Jellicoe, Harwood, Sturdee and Fisher already exist. The naming question is closed. What is open is what each house is for: which Sport, Uniformed Group and Performing Art it flies, which talent domain it stewards for the whole school, and how a Beattyian comes to belong to it. Model any combination, save it, and compare it against the others.

The through line, if you want one. Beatty's motto is Non Vi Sed Arte, not by force but by skill, and the school then named its houses after five men whose profession was force. That reads as a contradiction until you look at what each is actually remembered for. Fisher for engineering. Jellicoe for judgement and restraint. Hood for going first. Sturdee for preparation. Harwood for reading a situation while outnumbered. Not one of the five is remembered for force. Each is remembered for a form of skill. The house system does not need new names. It needs the existing names taken seriously.

The vertical column

A house is only vertical if it actually holds Beattyians at every level. This is where the CCA anchored model shows its weakness: CCAs thin at upper secondary, so a CCA anchored column narrows exactly where the Sec 3 and Sec 4 seniors who should be leading it are meant to be.
