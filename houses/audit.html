Enrichment activity audit

Every calendar item in the beattyadmin mailbox, 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025, opened, decomposed and classified. The Wednesday and Thursday 3pm to 6pm band is treated as the unit of interest; everything else is included for contrast.

What the enrichment band was actually used for

The band runs on a container card: a Level by banding-group table that departments fill in each week. Each filled cell is one entry. This is the honest picture of how the slot was spent, including the ways it was not spent on enrichment.
