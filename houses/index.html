House system, working tools

Beatty Secondary School. Four interactive models and the written record behind them, covering the enrichment band audit, the vertical house architecture, the capacity arithmetic that constrains both, and the leadership dashboard that couples the house system to talent development.

The through line. Beatty's motto is Non Vi Sed Arte, not by force but by skill, and the school then named its houses after five men whose profession was force. That reads as a contradiction until you look at what each is actually remembered for. Fisher for engineering. Jellicoe for judgement and restraint. Hood for going first. Sturdee for preparation. Harwood for reading a situation while outnumbered. Not one of the five is remembered for force. Each is remembered for a form of skill. The house system does not need new names. It needs the existing names taken seriously.

What the modelling settled

  • Five houses need five sports, and Beatty has four. MOE files Wushu under Visual and Performing Arts. Count it as a sport and the arithmetic closes exactly: five sports, five uniformed groups, six performing arts with one spare. File it as an art and one house has no sport anchor.
  • Class nesting is the binding constraint nobody names. Form classes per level must divide by five or the houses cannot hold whole classes evenly at every level. Eight does not divide; ten does. This decides itself at Sec 1 registration whether or not anyone models it first.
  • Vertical houses and inter class activity only reinforce under class anchoring. If a form class sits entirely in one house, every inter class competition at every level is already an inter house competition and the house table fills from events already running. Split the class across houses and that free scoring channel closes.
  • Gender balance and anchor reach actively fight. Levelling gender wants Volleyball beside a boys uniformed group; keeping flagships open to everyone wants them apart. Three boys only CCAs against one girls only is structural, not bad luck. The Studio's four solver buttons make the disagreement explicit.
  • Under class anchoring the anchors cannot balance anything. House size comes from class allocation and gender comes from the classes themselves. The anchors only control reach. Gender is balanced by house aware Sec 1 class allocation, not by which sport a house flies.
