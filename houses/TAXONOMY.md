# BTY Enrichment Taxonomy v1

Assign every atomic activity exactly ONE category and ONE sub-category, using the code.
If an activity plausibly fits two, use the PRIMARY INTENT of the activity, not its venue or provider.

## 1. STEM
- 1.1 Mathematics olympiad and contest — SMO Training, Singapore Math Kangaroo Contest (SMKC), Math Innovators
- 1.2 Science olympiad and competition — Physics Olympiad, science competitions
- 1.3 Applied STEM and NEXUS modules — NECTAR STEM Workshops, NX2xx/NX3xx modules, InnoSpace research, research process briefings, STEM workshops
- 1.4 Design, making and sustainability tech — composting workshop, upcycling workshop where the intent is applied/technical

## 2. Language Arts
- 2.1 English writing and literature — Creative Writing Programme, EL Writing Workshop, essay writing competitions and briefings, literature play viewing
- 2.2 Oracy, debate and performance — Debate Workshop, Debate Fest, Readers' Theatre Programme, EL Podcast Training, oracy workshops, EL oral timed practice (practice, not exam)
- 2.3 Mother Tongue enrichment — Comics Chinese Essay Writing Workshop, MTL Fortnight, MTL enrichment (NOT oral exams)
- 2.4 Language benchmarking and external tests — NIE comprehension test, benchmarking activity with external partner

## 3. Humanities and Social Science
- 3.1 Historical inquiry — Historical Investigation LJ, Historical AR Trail, History LJ, museum learning journeys with a History frame
- 3.2 Geographical inquiry — Geographical Investigation, Sustainable Neighbourhood GI, NUS Geography Challenge Training
- 3.3 Policy, economics and social studies — Raffles Policy Dialogue, EBS learning journey, National Cashflow Competition

## 4. Global and Civic Engagement
- 4.1 Model UN — MUN publicity talk, selection, training, mock conference
- 4.2 International exchange — NX3016 e-exchange with Saue School Estonia, NX3012 Mutsuzawa, overseas immersion
- 4.3 Environment and sustainability citizenship — National Youth Environment Conference, urban farm learning journeys, Live On Festival

## 5. Character, Leadership and Service
- 5.1 Peer Support Leader (PSL) training — all PSL briefings, training, appreciation activities
- 5.2 Student leadership development — Student Leadership Workshops, Class Committee Leader briefings, Tier Three Student Leaders, Sports Leaders selection, investiture rehearsals, NEXUS Award interview briefing
- 5.3 VIA and community service — all VIA projects and implementations
- 5.4 Cyber wellness and CCE advocacy — cyberwellness advocacy, CCE-framed sessions

## 6. Sports, Health and Outdoor
- 6.1 Fitness testing — NAPFA test and make-up NAPFA
- 6.2 Recreational and inter-class sport — Inter-class Games, Termly Sports Challenge, Termly Fitness Challenge, sports enrichment blocks
- 6.3 Outdoor and adventure education — MOE-OBS Challenge, cohort camps

## 7. Aesthetics and Performing Arts
- 7.1 Visual arts — Art Learning Journey, gallery visits
- 7.2 Performing arts — CNY concert, Chingay, drama, VPA COP

## 8. Education and Career Guidance
- 8.1 Post-secondary exposure — poly and ITE open houses, NYP course advising, EIP, Level Up
- 8.2 Industry and work exposure — PIL Industrial Attachment
- 8.3 Financial literacy

## 9. Uniformed Groups and CCA-linked
- 9.1 Uniformed group training — NCC air rifle, live firing, NCDCC and Scouts enrichment
- 9.2 Other CCA-linked enrichment

## 10. Non-enrichment use of the slot (flag, do not treat as enrichment)
- 10.1 Oral examinations — all MTL, HMTL, MTL-B and EL oral exams
- 10.2 Written examinations and prelims
- 10.3 ICT training and device rollout — Sec 1 ICT Training series, PLD rollout
- 10.4 Surveys, briefings and admin — DREAMS longitudinal survey, SEC survey, camp briefings, FGDs, registration
- 10.5 Slot held but unfilled — card exists, no level entry recorded
