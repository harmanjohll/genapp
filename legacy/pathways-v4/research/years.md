## P6 to Sec 1
- **AL bands (confirmed):** AL1 >=90, AL2 85-89, AL3 80-84, AL4 75-79, AL5 65-74, AL6 45-64, AL7 20-44, AL8 <20. Foundation A/B/C count as AL 6/7/8. The PSLE Score runs from 4 to 32.
- **Posting Groups (confirmed, MOE "What are posting groups"):**
  | PSLE Score | Group | Start level |
  |---|---|---|
  | 4-20 | PG3 | G3 |
  | 21-22 | PG2 or PG3 | G2 or G3 |
  | 23-24 | PG2 | G2 |
  | 25 | PG1 or PG2 | G1 or G2 |
  | 26-30 + AL7 or better in EL **and** Maths | PG1 | G1 |
  - In the option bands (21-22 and 25), the family picks one group, and it applies to all 6 school choices.
  - A score of 26-30 without AL7 in both EL and Maths, or a score of 31-32, gives no Posting Group.
- **Subject upgrades from Sec 1 (confirmed):** these apply to PG1 and PG2 students only, for EL, MTL, Maths and Science.
  - AL5 or better (Standard) lets the student take the subject at G3 (or G2 for a PG1 student).
  - AL6 (Standard) or AL A (Foundation) lets the student take the subject at G2.
  - A PG3 student who took a Foundation subject may take it at a lower level, after consulting the school.
- **Higher MTL (confirmed):** from the 2026 S1 intake, the student needs AL1 or AL2 in MTL, or Distinction or Merit in HMTL, whatever the overall score. The old rule was a score of 8 or better, or 9-14 plus that grade. Schools keep discretion to offer HMTL to others.
- **Timeline:**
  - 2025 cycle (confirmed): results and Option Form 25 Nov; choices 25 Nov to 1 Dec 4.30pm; posting results 19 Dec.
  - 2026 cycle (confirmed tentative): results 24 or 25 Nov 2026, with DSA results on the same day.
  - 2026 option window and posting date: not found.

## Sec 1 and Sec 2
- **Levelled subjects (confirmed):** EL, MTL, Maths, Science and Humanities are taken at G1/G2/G3. The Common Curriculum (Art, Music, D&T, FCE, PE, CCE) is taken in the mixed form class and is not levelled.
- **Humanities upgrades (confirmed):** Geography, History and Literature can be taken at a more demanding level from Sec 2, based on the student's aptitude and interest in Sec 1.
- **Level reviews:** MOE describes "various points", including the end of Sec 1 and the end of Sec 2 (confirmed). The criteria are school-based: subject performance plus a holistic assessment. There are no national thresholds.
  - Example school threshold: 75% end-of-year (Pei Cai). Treat it as an example only (provisional).
- **Moving down:** MOE says schools advise on "more or less demanding" levels (confirmed), but no criteria are published.
- **Lateral transfer:** still available (confirmed).

## Sec 2 to Sec 3 (mostly provisional, school-specific)
- MOE says upper-secondary students take 5-9 examinable subjects; about 7-8 is typical.
- The usual set is EL, MTL, Maths, a Science option, and Combined Humanities (Social Studies plus Geography, History or Literature), plus electives.
- For Science, students take Combined Science or 2-3 Pure Sciences.
- Allocation is by merit, choice order and class capacity.
- Schools set their own AMath bars. Examples found range from 55% to 70% in Sec 2 G3 Maths.
- **Applied Subjects (confirmed):** students can offer G3 MOE Applied Subjects (e.g. Computing, Exercise & Sports Science) and G1 MOE-ITE Applied Subjects (e.g. Smart Electrical Technology).
  - Elements of Business Skills is a G1 subject.
  - SEAB says 2027 is the last exam year for the MOE-ITE Applied Subjects.

## Sec 3 to Sec 4
- Schools can adjust levels "at appropriate junctures". No national rule was found for Sec 3 and Sec 4 (provisional).
- **SEC 2027 (confirmed):**
  - Orals, listening and practicals come first.
  - EL and all MTL written papers are in September, in one MTL sitting.
  - All other papers run October to November.
  - All results come out together in January 2028.
- **Early admissions (2026 dates for reference, confirmed):**
  - ITE EAE: 20-26 May.
  - Poly EAE for O-Level students: 25 Jun to 1 Jul.
  - DSA-JC: 6 May to 2 Jun.
- **PSE 2028 (confirmed):** it replaces the JAE, the PFP exercise and the ITE JIE, and anyone who sat the SEC at any level can take part.
  - Mid-January application window: 6 days, up to 12 choices (provisional).
  - Posting results: "early February" vs "end February". This conflict is unresolved.
- **2028 entry rules (confirmed):**
  - JC: L1R4 <= 16, all G3.
  - Poly Y1: ELR2B2 <= 22, with one B subject allowed at G2.
  - PFP: ELMAB3 (G2-equivalent) <= 12.
  - ITE 2-Year Higher Nitec: ELMAB3 (G2-equivalent) <= 19.

## Sec 5 (confirmed)
- Sec 5 continues for eligible students, so they can pace their learning or take subjects at a more demanding level.
- **Eligibility:** 2 or fewer G3 passes, and ELMAB3 <= 21, or ELB3 <= 14, or MAB3 <= 14, computed at G2 with Grade 5 or better in each subject counted.
- **All subjects at G3 in Sec 5:** needs ELMAB3 <= 19.

## Beatty Secondary (Toa Payoh)
- **Background:** non-affiliated co-ed government school, and a Full SBB school since 2022.
- **2025 PSLE ranges (SchoolFinder excerpt):** PG3 12-18, PG2 21-25, PG1 25-28. In 2024 PG3 was 12-17.
- **Subjects:**
  - Core: EL, Maths, AMath, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Science combinations.
  - Humanities: Humanities (SS + Geography or History), Geography, Literature.
  - Languages: CL, ML and TL with Higher and Basic variants.
  - Others: Art, Music, D&T, PE, Principles of Accounts, Nutrition & Food Science, Computer Applications.
  - Applied: Elements of Business Skills, Smart Electrical Technology.
  - SchoolFinder notes the list "may change"; G-levels are not listed.
- **ALP:** "Beattyians Think. Create. Innovate", STEM programming for smart living. The Principal's Message frames it as Machine Learning/AI.
- **LLP:** "Beatty's Leaders for Life Programme" (Community Youth Leadership).
- **DSA (provisional):** Leadership; Chinese Orchestra, Choir, Concert Band, English Drama, Malay Dance, Modern Dance; Badminton, Canoeing, Football (boys), Volleyball (girls), Wushu.
- **CCAs:** a partial list only (see JSON).

## Re-check 2026-11-01 (v4.2.0)

How: search engine extracts of moe.gov.sg pages; no page opened in full (not on this environment's allowed network list).

Held, unchanged: Posting Groups by PSLE Score; upgrades at entry (Standard AL 5 or better gives G3 for PG1 and PG2; AL 6 or Foundation A gives G2 for PG1); humanities move from Sec 2 on interest and aptitude in Sec 1; upper secondary 5 to 9 examinable subjects (school slides; one source says 5 to 7).

Corrected in the app:
- Higher Mother Tongue for the 2026 Sec 1 intake onwards: MTL AL 1 or 2, or Distinction or Merit in PSLE Higher MTL; the PSLE Score no longer counts. The P6 page now takes the HMTL result (MOE press release, 14 Sep 2024).
- Posting Group 3 with a Foundation subject: English, Maths or Science can be taken at G2; Foundation MTL A at G2, B or C at G2 or G1. An option with the school (MOE Full SBB FAQ).
- Timing: MOE sets no national dates for lower secondary level changes. Schools review at "appropriate junctures", on results and a holistic look at workload; students can ask and the school decides. The app now says "at your school's review, often at the end of Sec 1".
- Lower secondary is judged by its own rules: admission in 2028 reads only the SEC, and upper secondary adds exam subjects, so the last subject each place counts can come from Sec 3. Mother Tongue need not be G3 for JC or MI.

Still open: placement for scores 26 to 30 without AL 7 in English and Maths, and 31 to 32; HMTL entry after Sec 1; whether DSA JC or early admissions read lower secondary results.
