# E21CC: the competency lens behind the Learning Log

Research brief, September 2026, for the competency layer of `logs/`. The point of this document is to make sure the lens students see uses MOE's own names and definitions, so that the log, the SLS 21CC Navigator, EAGLES and the syllabus tables all speak one language.

## 1. The framework

From the centre outwards: **Core Values**; **Social-Emotional Competencies**; the outer ring of **Emerging 21st Century Competencies** (E21CC) in three domains; framed by the four **Desired Outcomes of Education**.

**Core Values [MOE].** "Knowledge and skills must be underpinned by values: Respect, Responsibility, Integrity, Care, Resilience and Harmony. Values form the core of the framework of 21st Century Competencies."

**Social-Emotional Competencies [MOE].** Five, interconnected: Self-Awareness, Self-Management, Social Awareness, Relationship Management, Responsible Decision-Making. MOE: "competencies necessary for children to develop healthy identities, recognise and manage their emotions, develop a sense of responsibility, care and concern for others, relate to others and develop positive relationships, handle challenges, make responsible decisions, and act for the good of self, others and the society."

**The outer ring [MOE].** "Building on a sound character foundation, the outer ring of Emerging 21CC enable students to thrive in and beyond school while living, learning and working in rapidly changing, highly digitalised and interconnected environments." E21CC "comprise knowledge, skills, dispositions and values that enable a person to meet complex demands in specific contexts."

| Domain | MOE's one line description [MOE] |
| --- | --- |
| Critical, Adaptive and Inventive Thinking | "enables students to use sound reasoning and metacognitive skills to inform decision-making, generate novel and useful ideas to address issues, and manage complexities and ambiguities to adapt to changing contexts with agility." |
| Communication, Collaboration and Information Skills | "enable students to communicate information, ideas and feelings clearly, engage in effective collaboration with others, and manage, create and share information thoughtfully, ethically and responsibly." |
| Civic, Global and Cross-Cultural Literacy | "enables students to contribute constructively to their community and nation, interact respectfully and empathically with others in diverse communities, and act as responsible citizens of Singapore and the world." |

### What changed in 2023

| Element | 2010 | 2023 refresh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thinking domain | Critical and Inventive Thinking | Critical, Adaptive and Inventive Thinking; Adaptive Thinking becomes a named competency |
| Communication domain | Communication, Collaboration and Information Skills | Name unchanged; rebuilt as three named competencies |
| Civic domain | Civic Literacy, Global Awareness and Cross-Cultural Skills | Civic, Global and Cross-Cultural Literacy: three literacies |
| Sub-structure | Four sub-components per domain | Three named competencies per domain, nine in all, each with a definition, Learning Goals and Developmental Milestones |
| Progression document | Standards and Benchmarks for E21CC (January 2014) | Learning Goals and Developmental Milestones at go.gov.sg/21cc |
| Recognition | EAGLES | EAGLES enhanced from 2024 to recognise E21CC |

Announced at the Schools Work Plan Seminar, 20 September 2023 [MOE]. The parliamentary reply of 2 July 2024 confirms "The 21CC Framework was last reviewed and updated in 2023" [MOE].

**Rationale, in MOE's words.** Press release, 20 September 2023: "To keep pace with rapid technological advancements and global developments, MOE will be enhancing the 21CC Framework, placing greater emphasis on adaptive and inventive thinking, communication, and civic literacy." Minister Chan at WPS 2023: "every student needs to be a creator, a connector and a contributor going forward"; the refresh is "foregrounding four competencies": adaptive thinking, inventive thinking, communication ("an in-depth understanding of others beyond ourselves") and civic literacy ("appreciating Singapore's unique context, appreciating our place in the world").

## 2. The nine competencies, as the app names them

These definitions appear identically in MOE syllabuses issued from 2024 and on MOE school pages reproducing MOE's EAGLES text. All **[MOE]**. The app quotes the definition (as "MOE: ...") once a student selects a competency, and pairs it with a plain "I ..." line written for Sec 1 to Sec 5.

**Critical, Adaptive and Inventive Thinking**

| Competency | MOE definition | App's plain line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical Thinking | "the ability to exercise sound reasoning and metacognitive thinking to interpret and analyse information and evidence, draw conclusions, make decisions, and solve problems." | I reason carefully with evidence before I decide, and I can say why. |
| Adaptive Thinking | "the ability to apply learnt knowledge and skills strategically and with flexibility in different or new and evolving contexts." | I use what I know in a new or changed situation, and I change my approach when the first one is not working. |
| Inventive Thinking | "the ability to frame, investigate and explore issues, generate innovative ideas, and evaluate them to form novel and useful responses." | I ask my own questions, try ideas that are not in the notes, and test whether they work. |

**Communication, Collaboration and Information Skills**

| Competency | MOE definition | App's plain line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communication | "the ability to convey information and exchange ideas clearly and coherently through multimodal ways for specific purposes, audiences and contexts." | I get ideas across clearly for the person in front of me, and I check they followed. |
| Collaboration | "the ability to work together in a respectful manner to share responsibilities and make collective decisions to meet shared goals." | I share the work and the decisions in a group, and I make room for others. |
| Information Skills | "the ability to source for, select, evaluate and synthesise digital and non-digital information with discernment. It also entails ethical and responsible practices when using, sharing and creating information." | I find, check and combine information, and I use it honestly and say where it came from. |

**Civic, Global and Cross-Cultural Literacy**

| Competency | MOE definition | App's plain line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civic Literacy | "the ability to understand the nation's values, governance, context and realities, form one's civic identity, and constructively engage with and contribute to one's community and nation." | I understand how Singapore works and I play my part in my community. |
| Global Literacy | "the ability to understand and think with discernment about world issues and interact responsibly and constructively with people from and beyond Singapore on such issues." | I connect this to what is happening in the wider world, and I deal fairly with people from anywhere. |
| Cross-Cultural Literacy | "the ability to sensitively understand, appreciate and interact with different social, cultural and religious communities and their perspectives." | I notice how people from different backgrounds might see this differently, and I respect it. |

For older school documents [Secondary], the 2010 sub-components map cleanly: sound reasoning and decision-making and reflective thinking sit under Critical Thinking; managing complexities and ambiguities under Adaptive Thinking; curiosity and creativity under Inventive Thinking; openness and communicating effectively under Communication; management and responsible use of information under Information Skills; active community life and national and cultural identity under Civic Literacy; global awareness under Global Literacy; socio-cultural sensitivity and awareness under Cross-Cultural Literacy.

## 3. Student outcomes

**Desired Outcomes of Education [MOE].** Confident person ("have a zest for life, have a strong sense of right and wrong, are adaptable and resilient, know themselves, are discerning in judgment, think independently and critically, and communicate effectively"); self-directed learner ("take responsibility for their own learning, are curious, reflective, and persevering in the lifelong pursuit of learning, driven by their passion and purpose"); active contributor ("are empathetic and open-minded to collaborate effectively in teams, exercise initiative, have courage to take risks responsibly, are innovative and strive for excellence"); concerned citizen ("are rooted to Singapore, have a strong civic consciousness, are responsible to their family, community and nation and take active roles in improving the lives of others").

**Key Stage Outcomes, end of secondary [MOE].** Students should: have moral integrity; believe in their abilities and be able to adapt to change; be able to work in teams and show empathy for others; be creative and have an inquiring mind; be able to appreciate diverse views and communicate effectively; take responsibility for own learning; enjoy physical activities and appreciate the arts; believe in Singapore and understand what matters to Singapore. These eight are the closest thing MOE publishes to a student worded end of secondary profile. Phase 2 candidate: a yearly "where am I against these eight" view in the Journey screen.

## 4. Progression: Learning Goals and Developmental Milestones

The 2014 Standards and Benchmarks (bands Approaching, Meeting, Exceeding, used in SEAB rubrics [Secondary]) were replaced in 2023 by Learning Goals and Developmental Milestones [MOE, from 2024 syllabuses]: "The Developmental Milestones clarify and specify possible indicators of development for each Learning Goal. Details of these Learning Goals and Development Milestones can be found via the link https://go.gov.sg/21cc." One secondary milestone was recovered, for Critical Thinking: students "using evidence and adopting different viewpoints to explain their reasoning and decisions, having considered the implications of the relationship among different viewpoints."

**Not verified:** the full end of secondary milestone set for all nine; whether go.gov.sg/21cc is public or staff only. **Not found:** any MOE student facing "I can" statements. The app's three levels (tried it, getting there, can teach it) are ours, deliberately coarse; if the school adopts the MOE milestones, they slot in as descriptors under each level.

## 5. MOE guidance that bears on a reflection log

- **SEAB, Assessment as Learning [MOE].** The March 2024 cluster workshop "Strengthening 21st Century Competencies through Assessment as Learning" aimed "to deepen participants' understanding of how AaL fosters self-awareness, self-reflection and self-regulation: crucial metacognitive processes"; in 2025 SEAB followed with "Involving Students in Assessment: Enhancing student outcomes in 21CC". Student self-assessment of 21CC is now an expected practice, not an experiment.
- **Revised A-Level Project Work, from 2024 [MOE].** Pass or Fail, with "an individual mark ... awarded for Individual Reflection". Reflection is assessed at the top of the system.
- **EAGLES in E21CC, from 2024 [MOE].** The award now recognises "consistent and exemplary demonstration of Emerging 21st Century Competencies", "as observed in school settings and contexts". A log with dated evidence per competency is exactly the paper trail a nomination needs.
- **CCE 2021 [MOE].** "takes reference from the existing Framework for 21st Century Competencies and Student Outcomes"; the inner two rings are taught through CCE, the outer ring through subjects, CCA, ALP and LLP. The ACT framework's first question ("How do I feel about the feedback?") is a Self-Awareness prompt in CCE's terms.
- **SLS 21CC Navigator [MOE].** "a gamified self-paced personal growth tool on SLS that makes 21st Century Competencies visible", with E21CC Quests (secondary module), a CAIT toolkit and E21CC tagged subject modules. MOE reports gains in awareness and motivation. The app uses the same nine names so the two do not compete.
- **Dispositions.** MOE uses "dispositions" alongside competencies ("knowledge, skills, dispositions and values"), not as a separate secondary list. **[Unverified]** No MOE list of secondary learning dispositions was found.

## 6. What the evidence says about reflecting on competencies rather than tasks

- NIE's Local Evidence Synthesis on 21CC (119 studies, 2009 to 2018) [Secondary]: what works is "fostering positive teacher attitudes, designing formative assessments, promoting collaborative learning, and leveraging ICT tools".
- Asia Society and NIE, 2017 [Secondary]: no large scale effectiveness studies; the standing impediment is a schooling culture that privileges "canonical disciplinary knowledge and academic achievement".
- My Groupwork Buddy (NIE, Koh et al.) [Secondary]: secondary students self and team assessed against targets set beforehand, then got feedback; the pattern that mattered was name the competency, set a target, rate, get feedback, repeat.
- SEAB's national 21CC rubrics relied on teacher judgement, not student self report, because self report scales inflate [Secondary].

**Design consequences in the app.**

1. Name the competency in MOE's words and give one observable line; the coach then asks for the moment ("Where in your answers does it show?"). Reflection on "how I evaluated the source" rather than "what we did".
2. Self placement is coarse (three levels) and always paired with a written moment, so it is evidence, not a mood.
3. Short and frequent: eight questions after a lesson, six after feedback, three sentences each. The exam term kills anything longer.
4. The coach is formative in SEAB's sense: a gift, a stretch, one question. No marks.
5. A "task talk" detector: when an entry is mostly task nouns (worksheet, test, marks) with no process verbs, the coach says so and asks what the student did with their mind.

## 7. Glossary for students (used in the app's Guide)

- **21CC**: the skills, attitudes and values MOE says you need for life and work, not just exams.
- **E21CC**: the outer ring; nine skills in three sets: thinking, communicating and working with others, being a citizen.
- **Core Values**: Respect, Responsibility, Resilience, Integrity, Care, Harmony.
- **Social-Emotional Competencies**: knowing yourself, managing feelings, understanding others, getting along, making good choices.
- **Metacognition**: thinking about your own thinking and how you learn.
- **Dispositions**: habits such as curiosity or perseverance; what you do when nobody is marking you.
- **Reflection**: looking back at how you thought and worked, not just what you made, and deciding what to change.
- **EAGLES**: the Edusave award that, from 2024, also recognises students who show E21CC consistently.
