For students

  • Write a GROW after a lesson or a CCA, or an ACT when feedback comes back.
  • The coach asks one question back, so your next entry goes further.
  • Two entries, in two different subjects or CCAs, two weeks apart, secure an I can statement.
  • Close it halfway and your words wait for you.

For teachers

  • Students choose what to share with you; nothing comes unless they do.
  • Reply in one tap, or a line of your own; it reaches the student through Teams.
  • See which entries are still waiting for a reply.
  • The console is locked with a PIN, and what it holds is encrypted on your device.

For parents

  • Your child’s log is kept on their own device.
  • Your child decides which teacher sees which entries; nothing is shared by default.
  • Teachers reply with short notes, and your child sees them first.
  • Ask your child to show you their wheel: it shows where they are growing.

Everyone in the demo is made up. To start a real log, use the link from your school.
