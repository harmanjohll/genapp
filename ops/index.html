Additional Guidelines for Activities when Air Quality is “Unhealthy” / “Elevated”

Outdoors?
Is the activity Strenuous1?
Prolonged2?
Answer the three questions above.

Notes:

  1. Strenuous activity causes your heart rate to increase significantly. Your breathing will get harder and faster and it will be more difficult to hold a conversation. The exercise tolerance of children may vary from child to child, based on their individual physical fitness and health condition. If a child feels unwell, he/she is to stop participating in the activity, be moved indoors (with air purifier) and seek medical attention, as appropriate to the severity of the child’s condition.
    1. Examples of strenuous activities are circuit training, jogging/running, mass PE involving aerobic-type exercises.
    2. Examples of non-strenuous activities are stretching, walking, static practice tasks, e.g., passing/shooting when stationary.
  2. Prolonged is defined as continuous exposure for several hours.
✎National ExaminationsO and A Level papers, MCs, rescheduling
MOE press conference, 16 Oct 2026
  • Students taking the O- and A-Level exams will sit for their papers in enclosed air-conditioned venues with air purifiers.
  • If they are unwell and unable to take or continue with an exam, they should see a doctor, obtain a medical certificate (MC), and apply for special consideration through their schools. Their schools will guide them on the necessary procedure.
  • Should the 24-hour PSI forecast reach hazardous levels, we will consider rescheduling O-Level papers in line with the health advisory for younger students.
  • The A-Level papers are generally less likely to be affected as the students are older, but we will also take reference from forecast as well as actual ground circumstances in making decisions on whether they will continue.
  • Details of any rescheduling of O- and A-Level papers to the earliest practicable date will be publicly announced by the Singapore Examinations and Assessments Board (SEAB).
  • Schools will also inform students and parents directly if any national exam needs to be rescheduled because of haze conditions.
◎How MOE decidesContinuity plans and precautionary measures
MOE press conference, 16 Oct 2026

MOE and Special Education (SPED) schools continuity plans

  • Available forecasts and advisories, as well as actual ground sitation will be taken into consideration to make an informed decision.
  • School leaders will take into account the 1-hour PM 2.5 readings and exercise judgement in managing school-based learning and activities.
  • Measures will be adjusted where needed to safeguard the health and well-being of our students and staff.
  • If there is a need to move nationally, MOE will make the announcement through our schools and official channels.

Our schools have already been taking precautionary measures

  • Adjusting activities
  • Deploying air purifiers
  • Paying extra attention to students who report to be unwell and/or have pre-existing health conditions
  • Taking practical steps to reduce exposure to haze where necessary

Junior Colleges, Millenia Institute and Post-Secondary Education Institutions

  • Junior Colleges (including the JC sections of Integrated Programme schools), Millennia Institute, ITE, polytechnics, Autonomous Universities and University of the Arts Singapore, because students are older, will generally continue in-person lessons.
  • We will also take reference from forecasts as well as actual circumstances on the ground in deciding whether to continue in-person activities or what measures should be adopted.

?How this app worksBands, regions, alerts
  • The band is set by whichever reading is worse: 24-hr PSI or 1-hr PM2.5.
  • Readings come from NEA via data.gov.sg. NEA updates hourly; this app checks every 10 minutes while open.
  • “Highest” takes the worst of the five regions.
  • Alerts: tap “Turn on alerts”. You are notified when the band changes, while the app is open. On iPhone, add Haze Ops to the Home Screen first.
  • Measures are transcribed from the MOE haze guidelines. Official announcements prevail.
