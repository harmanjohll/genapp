# Field test protocol

One class, 45 minutes, three instruments. The app collects nothing by design,
so paper is the instrument. Everything below fits one double-sided A4 for the
teacher and one slip per student.

## Before you start

- One device per student or pair. The site, loaded, on the Now screen.
- Print the slips (bottom of this file), cut, hand out face down.
- Recruit one observer if you can: a colleague, or yourself while a co-teacher
  runs the lesson. The observer never helps. Watching a student be stuck is
  the data.

## The 45 minute shape

| Min | What happens | What you watch |
|-----|--------------|----------------|
| 0 to 5 | Slips, side one. No preamble about the app. | Nothing yet. |
| 5 to 10 | "Set your subjects to what you actually take." Nothing else. | Who cannot find the level chips. Who asks a neighbour. |
| 10 to 25 | "Go to Journey. Play until 25 or further." Nothing else. | The moments below. |
| 25 to 35 | "Play again with a different want. Then put your two stories side by side." | Who finds Play again unaided. Who reads the compare screen aloud. |
| 35 to 42 | Pairs: "What did your starting point actually decide?" | Listen for the app's own words coming back: doors, moves, want. |
| 42 to 45 | Slips, side two. Collect. | Done. |

## The observation tally

Make a tick each time you see one of these. Five ticks on any row is a finding.

| Moment | What confused looks like |
|--------|--------------------------|
| First Journey screen | Reads everything, taps nothing, for more than 20 seconds |
| Want buttons | Asks "which one is right?" (the frame has failed if wants read as gradeable) |
| Turn screen | Taps a choice and waits, not finding Live this year |
| A move in the grid | Asks what "a real move" means, or whether it is real |
| Points | Tries to pick a third thing, does not understand why it will not take |
| Chance card | Asks whether they got it right or wrong |
| Lived screen | Skips it in under 2 seconds without reading (the beat is not landing) |
| Circles at 38 | Asks what the circles mean (the drawing has failed to self-explain) |
| Anywhere | Uses the word "score", "marks" or "win" |
| Anywhere | Shows a neighbour their screen unprompted (this is the good column) |

## The slips

Side one, before. Circle one number per line.

    I could name two places I could go after secondary school ... 1 2 3 4 5
    I know one thing I could do THIS YEAR that opens doors later ... 1 2 3 4 5
    If I wanted help with all this, I know who to ask ............. 1 2 3 4 5

Side two, after. Same three lines, plus one free line:

    One thing you found out today: ________________________________

Score movement per question, not per student. A rise on line two is the moves
system working. A rise on line three is the whole tool working, because the
tool's job is to end at a person.

## What to send back

Three numbers per question (before mean, after mean, n), the tally rows that
crossed five ticks, and the five most interesting free lines. That is enough
to steer the next revision, and it is evidence you can show a cluster.
